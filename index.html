FAQ

Questions, answered

Does DraftGuard read my passwords?

No — and it can't. Password fields and anything that looks like card numbers, CVV, OTP, PIN or IBAN data are excluded from capture at the code level. They are never stored, not even locally.

Where is my data stored?

Exclusively in Chrome's local extension storage on your own computer. DraftGuard makes zero network requests — there is no server that could see your text.

What happens after the free tier's 1 hour?

Drafts older than one hour are automatically deleted. Premium extends this to 30 days, and Vault-pinned drafts are kept forever.

Does it work on Gmail, Reddit, GitHub…?

Yes. DraftGuard supports plain text boxes, textareas, and rich contenteditable editors, and its restore mechanism is compatible with React/Vue-based sites.

Is DraftGuard open source?

No — DraftGuard is proprietary, closed-source software. This site and repository host the official documentation and releases only.

How do I cancel Premium?

Anytime, in two clicks from the extension popup. Your drafts stay on your device; retention simply returns to the free tier's window.
